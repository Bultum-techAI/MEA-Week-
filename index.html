How to Play
1

Sign in once

Type your name and pick your grade. Your stars are saved so you can come back any time.

2

Play the Break Missions

There are 4 days of missions — Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, and Day 4 — for your grade. Each day has 5 math and 5 reading questions about things your teachers taught this fall. Days unlock in order — finish one (and warm up in Math Facts) to open the next. You can play them whenever you like.

3

Earn points for everything

Each question is worth 10 points (middle school science & social studies questions are worth 5 points, half). In the Math Facts Arcade, single-digit facts are 1 point each and the double-digit Bonus Round is 2 points each. It all adds into one total score.

📚

Learn & Practice (free!)

Open Learn & Practice from your home screen. Read a short mini-lesson, watch a Khan Academy video, and practice as many times as you like. Practice does not use up your one try and does not earn points — it just makes you stronger.

🔬

Middle school: science & social studies

Middle school days include science and social studies questions worth half points (5 each). These questions may not be exactly what you learned in class — that is okay! Do your best, and use Learn & Practice to get ready.

⚠

One try per question — no redos!

You get one chance on each question. Once you answer, it's locked forever. Read carefully and choose your best answer!

🔓

Unlock the next day

The next day opens once you've finished the day before it and spent at least 2 minutes in the Math Facts Arcade. Keep practicing to keep climbing!

⚡

Math Facts Arcade — everyone plays!

Quick Facts earn 1 point each and match your grade: Kindergarten adds and subtracts within 5, 1st grade within 10, 2nd grade within 20, and 3rd grade practices × and ÷ facts. Older grades use single-digit facts. From 1st grade up you also unlock the Bonus Round (2 points each): within 20 in 1st grade, within 100 in 2nd, and two-digit numbers from 3rd grade up. Younger grades get more time. These points count toward your total and help unlock the next day.

🏆

Climb the leaderboard

Your question points and Math Facts points combine into one Total Points score. K–5 and Middle School each have their own leaderboard, side by side. The more you play, the higher you climb!

🎁

Win real prizes!

The Top 3 on each leaderboard (K–5 and Middle School) win a free prize box at Bultum — soccer balls, treats, and more. And the MEA Break Champion — the student at the top of each Total Points leaderboard — earns a FREE Cold Stone gift card!
